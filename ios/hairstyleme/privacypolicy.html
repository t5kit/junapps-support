📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "Hair Style Me" provided by the developer.

1. Information We Collect

Hair Style Me requires users to sign in with Apple in order to manage purchased credits and provide access across devices.

To generate AI hairstyle transformations, the app allows users to upload two photos:

  • A photo of the user
  • A hairstyle reference photo

These photos are securely transmitted to the AI image generation service solely for the purpose of generating the requested hairstyle transformation.

The developer does not permanently store uploaded photos or generated images on external servers.

2. Account and App Data

The app stores certain information to provide its functionality, including:

  • Sign in with Apple user identifier
  • Credit balance
  • Purchase history
  • App settings

This information is used only to provide the app's features, manage purchased credits, verify purchases, and maintain account access.

3. AI Hairstyle Generation

When you request a hairstyle transformation, your selected user photo and hairstyle reference photo are securely transmitted to an AI image generation service.

The images are used only to generate the requested hairstyle transformation.

The app is designed to preserve the user's facial identity and overall appearance while applying the hairstyle from the reference image.

The developer does not permanently store uploaded photos or generated images.

4. In-App Purchases

Hair Style Me uses a credit-based purchase system.

Credits can be purchased through Apple's App Store and are used to generate AI hairstyle transformations.

All payments are processed by Apple.

The developer does not receive or store payment card information.

Purchase verification is performed using Apple's StoreKit and App Store services.

5. Advertising

Hair Style Me does not display advertisements.

6. Analytics

Hair Style Me does not use third-party analytics services to track user behavior or collect usage statistics.

7. Third-Party Services

Hair Style Me uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple Sign in with Apple
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for processing and verifying in-app purchases
  • fal.ai for AI image generation

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

Hairstyle transformations are generated using artificial intelligence.

Results may vary depending on the quality, angle, lighting, hairstyle visibility, and other characteristics of the uploaded photos.

The app does not guarantee that every uploaded photo will produce a successful, accurate, or realistic result.

Generated results are intended as visual previews and should not be considered an exact prediction of how a hairstyle will look in real life.

Some images may not be processed due to safety restrictions imposed by the AI service.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information, purchase records, and credit balances are retained only as necessary to provide the app's functionality.

Uploaded photos and generated images are not permanently stored by the developer.

If you stop using the app, you may request deletion of your account data by contacting the developer.

10. Children's Privacy

Hair Style Me is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Some images involving children may not be processed due to safety restrictions imposed by the AI service.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any significant changes may be communicated through the app, the App Store listing, or the developer's website.
