Height Predictor

Height Predictor is a simple and easy-to-use app that estimates your future adult height based on your age, biological sex, current height, and your parents' heights.

Answer a few quick questions and receive a personalized adult height estimate, prediction range, and genetic growth summary in seconds.

The prediction is designed for educational and informational purposes and provides an easy way to explore how genetics may influence adult height.

This app is ideal for parents, teenagers, students, athletes, and anyone who is curious about their expected adult height.

Features

  • Estimate your future adult height
  • Simple questionnaire with only a few steps
  • Enter your age, biological sex, and current height
  • Include your father's and mother's reported heights
  • View your estimated adult height
  • See your predicted height range
  • View a personalized genetic height summary
  • Fast calculations with a clean interface
  • No account registration required
  • One-time purchase unlocks the full prediction report
  • No subscription required

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: What does this app do?
    A: Height Predictor estimates your future adult height using your age, biological sex, current height, and your parents' reported heights.
  • Q: Who is this app for?
    A: The app is designed for anyone who is curious about their expected adult height, including parents, teenagers, students, and athletes.
  • Q: What information do I need?
    A: You will be asked for your age, biological sex, current height, your father's height, and your mother's height.
  • Q: How long does it take?
    A: Most predictions can be completed in less than one minute.
  • Q: How is the prediction calculated?
    A: The estimate is based primarily on commonly used height prediction methods that consider biological sex and parental height. It is intended as an educational estimate rather than an exact prediction.
  • Q: Is the prediction guaranteed?
    A: No. Actual adult height can differ due to genetics, nutrition, health, medical conditions, development, and many other environmental factors.
  • Q: What is included in the full report?
    A: The full report includes your estimated adult height, prediction range, and a personalized genetic height summary.
  • Q: Is there a subscription?
    A: No. The app offers a one-time purchase that permanently unlocks the full prediction report.
  • Q: Can I restore my purchase?
    A: Yes. If you purchase the full version, you can restore it at any time using the Restore Purchases option.
  • Q: Does the app require an internet connection?
    A: An internet connection may be required to load purchase information or restore purchases. The prediction itself can be performed without creating an account.
  • Q: Is my information uploaded anywhere?
    A: No. Your answers are processed locally on your device. No account is required.
  • Q: Is this medical advice?
    A: No. Height Predictor is intended for informational and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice or replace professional healthcare guidance.
