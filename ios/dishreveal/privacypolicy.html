📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "DishReveal" provided by the developer.

1. Information We Collect

DishReveal requires users to sign in with Apple in order to manage purchased credits and provide access to the app's AI analysis features.

To analyze a dish, the app allows users to select or take a photo of food.

The selected food photo is securely transmitted to a remote AI service solely for the purpose of performing the requested food analysis.

The analysis may include:

  • Likely dish name and description
  • Likely visible ingredients
  • Possible ingredients
  • Estimated ingredient amounts
  • Estimated calories
  • Possible allergens
  • Suggested cooking steps

The developer does not permanently store uploaded food photos on external servers.

2. Account and App Data

The app stores certain information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Sign in with Apple user identifier
  • Credit balance
  • Purchase history
  • App settings

This information is used only to provide the app's features, manage purchased credits, verify purchases, and maintain account access.

Dish history and related information may be stored locally on the user's device, including:

  • Food photos saved to dish history
  • AI analysis results
  • Favorites
  • Cooking status
  • Categories
  • Tags
  • Memos
  • User-created My Recipes

3. AI Food Analysis

When you request a food analysis, the selected food photo is securely transmitted to a remote AI service.

The image is used to generate the requested analysis, such as likely ingredients, calorie estimates, possible allergens, and cooking guidance.

The developer does not permanently store the uploaded food photo on external servers.

AI analysis is based on visual information and general knowledge about similar dishes. It cannot reliably determine every hidden ingredient, exact quantity, cooking method, or preparation process.

4. Dish History and My Recipes

DishReveal allows users to save analyzed dishes to a personal history on their device.

Saved history may include the original food photo, AI analysis results, favorites, status, categories, tags, memos, and other organizational information.

Users can also create and save their own My Recipes, including custom recipe names, ingredients, amounts, and cooking steps.

This dish history and My Recipe information is stored locally by the app and is used to provide the app's organization and recipe-management features.

5. In-App Purchases

DishReveal uses a credit-based purchase system.

Credits can be purchased through Apple's App Store and are used to perform AI food analyses.

All payments are processed by Apple.

The developer does not receive or store payment card information.

Purchase verification is performed using Apple's StoreKit and App Store services.

6. Advertising

DishReveal does not display advertisements.

7. Analytics

DishReveal does not use third-party analytics services to track user behavior or collect usage statistics.

8. Third-Party Services

DishReveal uses third-party services necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Apple Sign in with Apple
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for processing and verifying in-app purchases
  • fal.ai and its underlying AI services for food image analysis

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. AI Analysis Disclaimer

Food analysis results are generated using artificial intelligence.

Results may vary depending on the quality of the image, lighting, angle, portion visibility, presentation of the dish, and other characteristics of the uploaded photo.

The app does not guarantee that every uploaded photo will produce a complete or accurate analysis.

Dish names, ingredients, ingredient amounts, calorie estimates, possible allergens, and cooking instructions are estimates and may differ from the actual dish.

Hidden ingredients, exact portion sizes, seasonings, sauces, oils, preparation methods, and cross-contact cannot be reliably determined from a photo.

Possible allergen information must not be used to determine whether a food is safe for someone with a food allergy or other medical condition.

The app is not a substitute for professional medical, nutritional, dietary, or allergy-safety advice.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information, purchase records, and credit balances are retained only as necessary to provide the app's functionality.

Uploaded food photos are not permanently stored by the developer on external servers.

Dish history, saved food photos, memos, tags, and My Recipes may remain stored locally on the user's device until the user deletes them or removes the app.

If you stop using the app, you may request deletion of account-related data held by the developer by contacting the developer.

11. Children's Privacy

DishReveal is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Users should upload food photographs intended for food analysis and should avoid including unnecessary personal information in uploaded images.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any significant changes may be communicated through the app, the App Store listing, or the developer's website.
