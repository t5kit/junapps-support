📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application “Farm Machine Log” provided by the developer.

1. Information We Collect

Farm Machine Log does not require account registration and does not ask users to enter personal information for account creation.

The app allows users to record information about farm machines, maintenance tasks, service history, operating hours, costs, notes, and optional photos. These values are used only for app functionality within the app and are not sent to the developer.

The app does not collect, store, or share farm machine records, maintenance task data, machine logs, hour meter values, costs, notes, photos, or CSV export contents on external servers operated by the developer.

2. Local App Data

Farm Machine Log stores app data locally on the user’s device.

This may include:

  • Farm machine names
  • Machine categories
  • Manufacturer and model information
  • Machine memos
  • Representative machine photos
  • Maintenance tasks
  • Maintenance intervals
  • Next due hour values
  • Last completed hour values
  • Machine logs
  • Dates
  • Hour meter values
  • Costs
  • Log notes
  • Attached log photos

This local data is used to display, edit, organize, and summarize farm machine information and maintenance history. The developer does not receive or store this data on external servers.

3. Farm Machine Records

Farm Machine Log allows users to manually create and manage farm machine records.

Machine information may include the machine name, category, manufacturer, model, memo, representative photo, latest recorded hour meter, total recorded cost, maintenance tasks, and machine logs.

The developer does not automatically receive, access, review, or analyze the farm machine information entered by users.

4. Maintenance Tasks

Users may create maintenance tasks for individual farm machines.

Maintenance task data may include task names, maintenance intervals, next due hour values, last completed hour values, and optional notes.

The app may calculate maintenance status based on the latest recorded hour meter and the configured next due hour.

Maintenance task data is stored locally on the user’s device and is not sent to the developer.

5. Machine Logs

Farm Machine Log allows users to manually create work, fuel, maintenance, repair, inspection, and other machine logs.

Each log may include a date, log type, title, hour meter, cost, memo, and optional photo.

When a maintenance task is completed, the app may update the task’s last completed hour and next due hour and may create a maintenance log based on the completed task.

The developer does not automatically receive, upload, review, or store machine log data on external servers.

6. Photos

Users may choose photos from their device to use as representative machine photos or attachments to machine logs.

Selected photos are copied to local app storage on the user’s device for use within the app.

The developer does not automatically receive, upload, or store selected photos on external servers.

Photos remain under the user’s control and may be changed or removed through the app where supported.

7. CSV Export

Farm Machine Log allows users to export machine log data and maintenance task data as CSV files.

CSV files may include information such as machine name, category, manufacturer, model, dates, log types, titles, hour meter values, costs, maintenance task names, intervals, next due hours, last completed hours, status, and notes.

CSV files are created locally on the user’s device and are shared only when the user chooses to use the iOS file export or sharing interface.

The developer does not automatically receive, upload, or store exported CSV files.

8. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Farm Machine Log offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions for Pro features.

Pro features may include:

  • Unlimited farm machines
  • Unlimited maintenance tasks
  • All-machine CSV export

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

The developer does not receive or store users’ payment card information.

Subscription status and purchase entitlements are verified through Apple’s StoreKit services.

9. Third-Party Services

Farm Machine Log uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for processing and verifying in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Apple system photo selection services for allowing users to choose photos from their device
  • Apple system file export and sharing services for exporting and sharing CSV files at the user’s request

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and platform rules.

10. Equipment and Maintenance Disclaimer

Farm Machine Log is a record-keeping and maintenance tracking tool.

The app does not automatically connect to farm machinery, telematics systems, sensors, diagnostic systems, or manufacturer service databases.

Hour meter values, maintenance intervals, service history, costs, and related information are entered manually by users.

The app is not a replacement for manufacturer service manuals, safety instructions, inspection procedures, or professional maintenance advice.

Users are responsible for verifying the accuracy of entered information and for following the maintenance and safety recommendations provided by equipment manufacturers and qualified professionals.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Farm machine records, maintenance tasks, machine logs, photos, and related app data remain stored locally on the user’s device until the user edits or deletes them, clears app data, or removes the app.

Deleting a machine or log may also delete associated photo copies stored within the app.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored Farm Machine Log data unless the operating system or another user-controlled backup method preserves it.

The developer cannot recover locally stored farm machine data because the developer does not receive or store that data on external servers.

12. Children’s Privacy

Farm Machine Log is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer so that appropriate action can be taken.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time.

Any significant changes may be communicated through the app, the App Store listing, or the developer’s website.
