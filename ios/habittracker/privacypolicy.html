📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application “Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks” provided by the developer.

1. Information We Collect

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks does not require account registration and does not ask users to enter personal information for account creation.

The app allows users to create and track habits by entering habit names, emojis, completion history, and reminder settings. These values are used only for app functionality within the app and are not sent to the developer.

The app does not collect, store, or share any habit names, completion history, reminder settings, progress data, streak data, or other habit tracking contents on external servers operated by the developer.

2. Local App Data

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks stores app data locally on the user’s device, including habits, completion dates, streak-related data, and reminder settings.

This local data is used to display, edit, and track habits, show progress, calculate streaks, and manage reminders. The developer does not receive or store this data on external servers.

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks may also store simple app data locally on the user’s device, such as whether Pro features are active through an in-app purchase or subscription.

3. Habits, Progress, and History

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks allows users to create habits and track daily completion history.

Habit names, emojis, completion dates, progress, streaks, and related history are stored locally on the user’s device. The developer does not automatically receive, access, or review the content entered by users.

4. Reminders and Notifications

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks allows users to set optional daily reminders for habits.

Reminder settings, such as reminder time and whether reminders are enabled, are stored locally on the user’s device. Notifications are scheduled as local notifications on the device.

The developer does not receive reminder contents or notification history.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks offers optional in-app purchases or subscriptions for Pro features, such as unlimited habit creation and removing ads.

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store users’ payment card information.

6. Advertising

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks may display banner ads using Google AdMob.

Third-party advertising services may collect and use certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Users may remove banner ads through an optional Pro subscription or in-app purchase.

7. Third-Party Services

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob for displaying ads
  • Apple App Store for processing in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Apple UserNotifications for local habit reminders

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and platform rules.

8. Health and Wellness Disclaimer

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks is a simple habit tracking and routine management tool. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or professional health guidance.

Users should consult a qualified professional for medical, mental health, fitness, or other health-related advice.

9. Children’s Privacy

Daily Habit Tracker: Streaks is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated through the app or via the distribution platform.
