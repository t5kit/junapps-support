Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: What does this app do?
    A: It transforms your photo into an AI-generated Samurai or Geisha portrait inspired by traditional Japanese history and culture.
  • Q: How do I use the app?
    A: Select a photo, choose either Samurai or Geisha, and the AI will generate your portrait in about one minute.
  • Q: What kind of photo works best?
    A: Clear front-facing photos with good lighting generally produce the best results.
  • Q: Will the AI keep my face recognizable?
    A: Yes. The AI is designed to preserve your facial identity while transforming your appearance into the selected historical style.
  • Q: How long does generation take?
    A: Most portraits are generated in approximately one minute, depending on server availability.
  • Q: How do credits work?
    A: Each portrait generation uses one credit. Credits can be purchased whenever you need them.
  • Q: Is there a subscription?
    A: No. The app uses a credit-based system with no recurring subscription.
  • Q: Can I restore my purchases?
    A: Yes. Purchased credits can be restored using the Restore Purchases option when available.
  • Q: Does the app require an internet connection?
    A: Yes. An internet connection is required because AI image generation is performed securely on remote servers.
  • Q: Are my photos stored permanently?
    A: Photos are used only to generate your portrait and are not stored permanently by the app.
  • Q: Can children use this app?
    A: The app is intended for teenagers and adults. AI-generated results involving young children may be limited or unavailable depending on safety policies.
