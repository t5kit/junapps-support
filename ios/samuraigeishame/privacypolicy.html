📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "Samurai & Geisha Me" provided by the developer.

1. Information We Collect

Samurai & Geisha Me requires users to sign in with Apple in order to manage purchased credits and provide access across devices.

To generate AI portraits, the app allows users to upload a photo and choose a transformation style.

The uploaded photo is securely transmitted to the AI image generation service solely for the purpose of generating the requested portrait.

The developer does not permanently store uploaded photos or generated portraits on external servers.

2. Account and Local App Data

The app stores certain information to provide its functionality, including:

  • Sign in with Apple user identifier
  • Credit balance
  • Purchase history
  • App settings

This information is used only to provide the app's features and manage purchased credits.

3. AI Image Generation

When you request a portrait, your selected photo is securely transmitted to an AI image generation service.

Your photo is used only to generate the requested Samurai or Geisha portrait.

The developer does not permanently store uploaded photos or generated images.

4. In-App Purchases

Samurai & Geisha Me uses a credit-based purchase system.

Credits can be purchased through Apple's App Store and are used to generate AI portraits.

All payments are processed by Apple.

The developer does not receive or store payment card information.

Purchase verification is performed using Apple's StoreKit services.

5. Advertising

Samurai & Geisha Me does not display advertisements.

6. Analytics

Samurai & Geisha Me does not use analytics services to collect usage statistics.

7. Third-Party Services

Samurai & Geisha Me uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple Sign in with Apple
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for processing and verifying in-app purchases
  • fal.ai for AI image generation

These services process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

Portraits are generated using artificial intelligence.

Results may vary depending on the uploaded photo and are intended for entertainment and creative purposes only.

The app does not guarantee that every uploaded photo will produce a successful or accurate result.

Some images, including those involving young children or content restricted by safety policies, may not be processed.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information, purchase records, and credit balances are retained only as necessary to provide the app's functionality.

Uploaded photos are not permanently stored by the developer.

If you stop using the app, you may request deletion of your account data by contacting the developer.

10. Children's Privacy

Samurai & Geisha Me is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

AI-generated portraits involving young children may be unavailable due to safety restrictions imposed by the AI service.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any significant changes may be communicated through the app, the App Store listing, or the developer's website.
