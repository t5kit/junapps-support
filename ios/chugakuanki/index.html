中学受験 理科・社会 一問一答

中学受験 理科・社会 一問一答 is a multiple-choice quiz app that helps elementary school students (grades 4–6) memorize science and social studies for Japanese junior high school entrance exams.

Questions answered incorrectly on the first try come back in the "Review" section three days later. Parents can check the number of questions answered, accuracy, study streak, and weak units on the "For Parents" screen.

Features

  • Four-choice quizzes for each science and social studies unit (10 questions per round)
  • A round does not end until all 10 questions are answered correctly
  • Questions answered incorrectly appear in the subject's "Review" section three days later
  • Questions answered correctly in a row during review are marked as "Learned"
  • Study records for parents: questions answered today, accuracy, study streak, a 7-day chart, weak units, and records for each unit
  • Furigana (reading aids) on questions and choices
  • Works on iPhone and iPad
  • No ads and no account registration. Study records are stored only on your device

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: Is the app free?
    A: Yes. 10 units are completely free, including review. Purchasing "Unlock All Units" (800 yen, one-time purchase) unlocks every unit, including units added in the future.
  • Q: Is there a monthly fee?
    A: No. "Unlock All Units" is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • Q: What happens if I change devices or reinstall the app?
    A: Tap "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen to restore "Unlock All Units" bought with the same Apple Account. Study records are stored only on the device, so they are not transferred to a new device.
  • Q: Can my family use it on their devices?
    A: Yes. The purchase supports Family Sharing. On a family member's device, tap "Restore Purchases."
  • Q: How are the questions made?
    A: Every question is checked against pages from public institutions such as the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan, the Japan Meteorological Agency, the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan, the Ministry of Education (MEXT), and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries. Numbers that change every year (such as production rankings) are not used.
  • Q: What should I do if I find a mistake in a question?
    A: Please contact us using the information below, and include the unit name and the question text. We will check it and fix it.
  • Q: Is history included?
    A: History is in preparation.
  • Q: Where are study records stored?
    A: Only on your device. They are never sent to a server. Deleting the app also deletes the records.
  • Q: Can I use the app offline?
    A: Yes. Solving questions and viewing records work offline. An internet connection is required to purchase or restore purchases.
  • Q: Do I need to create an account?
    A: No.
