📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the iOS application “中学受験 理科・社会 一問一答” provided by the developer.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Your study records (questions answered, correct and incorrect answers, study days, and review schedule) and app settings are stored locally on your device only. They are not transmitted to the developer's servers or any external servers. Deleting the app also deletes these records.

2. Third-Party Services

This app does not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

In-app purchases ("Unlock All Units") and purchase restoration are processed through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and the developer does not receive it. For more information, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for elementary school students and their parents. We do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated via the app or through the App Store page where the app is distributed.
